RS Composite—a USGBC GOLD CERTIFIED knitwear manufacturing facility — resides at the heart of Bangladesh's knitwear manufacturing hub, Narayanganj. It has developed an eco-friendly project strictly conforming to the Gold Category requirements in Leadership in Energy & Environmental Design, which are set by the US Green Building Council. From start to finish, RS has peerless standard, where quality is uncompromised. Since their inception in 1998, it’s been carrying out its responsibilities towards its people and the planet, while transcending through paramount customer contentment, nonetheless.

RS Composite dreams of a journey — a journey of long-lasting relationship and a network across the world, with the cardinal aim of customer satisfaction. It seeks a sustainable growth, keeping its workers, clients and Mother Nature in mind.

Green Feature:

Keeping its commitment to the environment, the factory building is surrounded by greenery and fountains of water.

RS takes responsibility for a habitable future, doing everything to limit carbon emission. The whole factory is integrated so as to achieve eco-sustainability.

Environment friendly techniques and technologies—like keeping room temperature below 30 degree Celsius for a comfortable working environment, rain water harvesting and usage, utilization of daylight, solid waste & waste water management, energy-efficient and noise-free machines etc.—are used to fulfill RS Composite's devotion to diminish its harm towards nature.

Most of the workers are advantaged by the ease of going to work on foot. With its strategic location comes the advancement of the local livelihood — crediting RS for helping its locality.

HR Compliance:

Regular training is organized to keep the workforce prepared to respond to Occupational Hazards and Fire Safety. Water hose, auto water pump, emergency lamp, PA system, fire alarm siren, fire exit and staircase are installed and maintained properly.

Finger Print
The workforce is the backbone of RS Composite. For their key contribution behind its success, they are given prime priority here. They receive free Healthcare and medicines. Keeping their children at the childcare service that RS provides, the workers can work ceaselessly.

They are provided pure drinking water. Proper industrial ventilation creates a healthy atmosphere for the workers. It strictly follows the local labor laws along with those set by ILO. At lunch and tea breaks, workers enjoy an entire floor dedicated for their comfort. Hand-washing station with auto-shower is dedicated for them. Workers are free to walk in the garden during breaks.

Their rights and responsibilities are established at the factory with Workers’ Participation Committee that develops awareness among the employees.

Knitting:

With contemporary, state-of-the-art open-width and tubular knitting machines, RS Composite produces premium knit fabrics. Like Single JERSEY, INTER LOCK, RIB, DROP NEEDLE, JACQUERED, LYCRA jersey, Pique, Double Lacoste, Slub Jersey, Double Jersey, Neppy yarn jersey, Inkjet yarn jersey etc.

Dyeing, Finishing & Lab:

Knitted fabrics are dyed and finished with world-class equipment. 4-Point Checking System is in place, for quality checking of each roll of fabrics, yard by yard. Here, knit dyeing quality is ensured using contemporary and well-calibrated laboratory facilities.
