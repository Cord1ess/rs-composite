### Environment

Heading: **Environment**

Most factories add sustainability afterwards. RSComposite was built around it.
Rainwater is harvested and reused. Effluent is treated chemically and
biologically before it returns to nature. The boiler that raises steam for the
finishing section burns the factory's own garment waste. Floors stay below 30°C.
Daylight does the work that lighting would. Most of the workforce walks to work.

### Quality

Heading: **Quality**

Every roll of fabric passes a 4 Point inspection before it becomes a garment.
The dye lab is calibrated. A quality control team follows the product from
knitting through to packing rather than only at the end. When a buyer wants to
see something, management reviews it with them over video, live on the floor.
**[I]**

### People

Heading: **People**

2,250 people work here and half of them are women. Healthcare and medicines are
free. Childcare is on site, so parents can work without dividing their
attention. There is drinking water and industrial ventilation. A whole floor is
set aside for lunch and tea. A hand washing station with an auto shower is
dedicated to the workforce. Rights and responsibilities are set out through a
Workers' Participation Committee.
Local labour law and ILO standards are followed. **[I][M]**

Safety block:

Fire safety is trained for rather than assumed. Water hoses, auto water pumps,
emergency lamps, a public address system, alarm sirens and dedicated fire exits
and staircases are installed and maintained. Regular training keeps the
workforce ready for occupational hazards. **[I]**

## Banned phrases

| Do not write | Why | Write instead |
|---|---|---|
| "the greenest factory in the world" **[M]** | Unverifiable superlative. A CSR officer discounts everything after it. | "LEED Gold certified by the USGBC" |
| "world class" on its own | Says nothing | Name the machine, standard or number |
| "woven" for knit goods | Technically wrong and buyers notice | "knitted", "knit" |
| "peerless", "unequaled", "utmost contentment" | Source document register. Reads as translated marketing. | Plain statements of fact |
| "state of the art" | Filler | The actual capability |
| "solutions", "leverage", "seamless", "unlock", "elevate" | Empty | The specific thing |
| "Our Journey", "Our Story", "Our Ecosystem" as page names | Hides what the page is | "About", "Sister Concerns" |
